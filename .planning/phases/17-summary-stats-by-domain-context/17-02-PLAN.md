<objective>
Wave 1 (spec §5). Build the extended analysis dataset per locked decision D-01, match every
column against the PRECEDE dictionary, assign each documented variable to exactly one clinical
domain (D1-D5) or OUT_OF_SCOPE, and record a one-line domain_rationale for every assigned
variable citing which of the three assignment rules applied. Persist the result as
g.var_domain_map and export it for Gerard's variable-by-variable review (Checkpoint 1). No
statistics run until that review is approved.

Purpose: The domain map is the spine of the whole phase. A wrong domain or a blank rationale
propagates into every downstream sheet. D-01's join is where the CHAR $12 vs NUM8 key conflict
must be neutralized or D3/frailty columns come back silently all-missing.
Output: sas/17_summary_stats_by_domain.sas Sections 1-4, g.var_domain_map, and
qc/17_var_domain_map_review.csv. Ends at a blocking human checkpoint.
</objective>
