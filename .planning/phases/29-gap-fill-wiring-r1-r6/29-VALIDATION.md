## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** Verify SAS log for ERROR/WARNING lines and `%assert_eq` output
- **After every plan wave:** Run the programs modified in that wave; check QC outputs under `qc/`
- **Before `/gsd:verify-work`:** Full `run_pipeline.cmd` must complete with no ERRORs
- **Max feedback latency:** ~5 minutes (per-program SAS runs)
