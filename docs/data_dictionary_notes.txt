SECTION 1 -- Deliberate multi-column concepts (PCM-D-01, PCM-D-02, PCM-D-03)
===========================================================================

The following concepts appear as multiple columns in g.master_data_merged BY DECISION,
not by oversight. See docs/DECISIONS.md "Resolutions -- 2026-08-27" for full rationale.

--- CONCEPT: Mortality flag (PCM-D-01, keep separate) ---

Columns in the merged file:
  Death_Date_Y_N     -- sources md1, md2, md3, md4, md5
  IsDead_Y_N         -- source md6
  Death              -- source md7

These are three source-specific column names for what may or may not be the same
underlying concept. They are NOT collapsed into one column because nobody has verified
they measure the same thing. Collapsing would assert equivalence that has not been proven.

Additional columns that are SEPARATE MEASURES (never merge candidates):
  _30_DAY_MORTALITY          -- sources md1, md2
  Death_Days_After_Surgery   -- sources md3, md4, md5, md6, md7

These are distinct constructs and must not be treated as variants of Death_Date_Y_N.

Analyst guidance: use in_md1 through in_md8 provenance flags to determine which
mortality column applies to a given patient's source record.

--- CONCEPT: Frailty components (PCM-D-02, keep separate) ---

Five frailty items exist in two encodings across sources:

  Character Y/N columns (sources md6, md7 -- $1 or $3):
    Feels_Exausted
    Low_Physical_Activity
    Slow_Walking_Speed
    Unintended_Weight_Loss
    Week_Grip_Strength

  Numeric _Value columns (sources md3, md5):
    Feels_Exausted_Value
    Low_Physical_Activity_Value
    Slow_Walking_Speed_Value
    Unintended_Weight_Loss_Value
    Week_Grip_Strength_Value

Total: ten columns for five concepts. All ten are retained in the merged file.

The width difference ($3 in md7 vs $1 in md6) is itself evidence that the two encodings
are not interchangeable -- reconciling them without knowing why they differ would be
guessing. The md7 width signal triggered the explicit ownership override at merge time.

--- CONCEPT: ISO_SEV exposure (PCM-D-03, keep separate) ---

Columns in the merged file:
  ISO_SEV_Exp_IntraOp_MAC_Average   -- sources md1, md2, md3
  ISO_SEV_IntraOp_MAC_Average       -- source md4
  ISO_SEV_MAC_TOTAL_Exp             -- source md8

CRITICAL: ISO_SEV_MAC_TOTAL_Exp (md8) is a TOTAL, not an average. It was never a naming
variant of the other two columns and must remain separate on that basis alone. The md4
name may be a variant of the md1-md3 name, but that is unverified.

SECTION 2 -- Emergent caveat (PCM-D-04, retained despite rarity)
===========================================================================

Emergent (PCM-D-04, retained): values are Y / N / blank. The merged value is md3-owned;
see qc/06_reconcile_summary.txt for the observed distribution. At source, md1 and md8
showed 0.05% and 0.09% Y with blank shares matching to within a tenth of a percent --
consistent across two independent exports, and consistent with a field clinicians rarely
complete rather than a true emergency rate. Patient_Type and Admit_Source are likely
better urgency proxies. Do not model on Emergent without checking the observed rate.

Note on source counts: the positive-Y counts from PCM-F-06 are SOURCE-LEVEL figures
describing md1 and md8 individually. They do NOT describe the merged Emergent column,
which is md3-owned. The merged distribution is in qc/06_reconcile_summary.txt.

SECTION 3 -- rt_envelope_flag (PCM-D-08 / MRG-05)
===========================================================================

Column: rt_envelope_flag
Type: numeric flag (0 / 1)
Source: derived in Phase 4 merge DATA step (not in the ownership map; excluded from
        the MRG-04 reconciliation per MRG-05 design)

rt_envelope_flag = 1 marks rows where an operative sub-interval exceeds the room-occupancy
interval (rt_RM_START_to_RM_END_mins) that contains it -- an impossible combination. At
least one of the three contributing timestamps must be wrong, but nothing in the data
identifies which one.

Resolution (PCM-D-08): VALUES ARE RETAINED, NOT NULLED. Nulling would destroy two good
values to punish one unidentifiable bad one. The flag moves the judgment to the analyst.

The flagged count (9 rows at the 2026-08-27 run) is an observation, not a fixed invariant.
Future re-extracts from the source system may produce a different count.

QC-06 asserts zero UNFLAGGED envelope violations (not zero violations overall). This
assertion will fire if a future re-extract introduces a violation the flag logic misses.

Analyst guidance: decide how to handle rt_envelope_flag = 1 rows before using operative
interval variables. Options include exclusion, sensitivity analysis, or retention with note.

Upstream note (logged for reporting): the source system emits impossible operative
timestamp combinations. The flag makes the pipeline honest; it does not fix the extract.

SECTION 4 -- Deferred and closed decisions
===========================================================================

--- Age floor (PCM-D-07, DEFERRED to Phase 7) ---

The observed minimum Age_at_Encounter in g.master_data_merged is 64. This was noted
but not investigated in Phase 6.

The QC-05 floor of 18 is a TYPE-SANITY GUARD only -- it exists to catch a numeric
conversion failure, not to define the analytic cohort. It cannot fire given the observed
minimum of 64.

DO NOT tighten the QC-05 floor to 64. That would convert an unexamined question into
a pipeline abort and embed an analytic decision in the QC layer.

Phase 7 (Cohort and Missingness) inherits this question when defining the analytic cohort.
See ROADMAP Phase 7 -- the cohort inclusion criterion discussion (PCM-D-05) is the
appropriate place to address the age floor.

--- Operative-interval ceilings (PCM-D-09, DROPPED) ---

The three QC-05 upper-bound assertions:
  rt_INCISE_to_DRESS_mins       ceiling 2000
  rt_RM_START_to_INCISION_mins  ceiling 500
  rt_RM_START_to_RM_END_mins    ceiling 2000

...never fired on any of 41,150 rows and were removed (QC-07). QC-05 now carries five
assertions (floors only for the operative intervals). Floors are handled at source by
PREP-08 (negatives set to missing); containment is enforced by QC-06 (envelope check).
A bound that has never fired is not a check. The distribution report is retained as the
record justifying this removal.

--- md3-owns missingness (PCM-D-11, CLOSED) ---

Variables owned by md3 inherit md3's missingness pattern in the merged file. Where md3
carries a blank on a variable it owns, no other source has a non-missing value for that
patient (PCM-F-17). This was verified for:

  Admit_BMI       -- md3 vs all seven other sources: 0 recoverable values
  Cognitive_Score -- md3 vs md5, md3 vs md6: 0 recoverable values
  Frailty_Score   -- md3 vs md5, md3 vs md6: 0 recoverable values

These three variables drive the 6,523 complete-case N for the primary analysis.

Caveat: "zero recoverable" means no other source has a value for the same patient where
md3 is blank. It does not mean the patient has no missingness -- it means the missingness
cannot be reduced by drawing from another source in this extract.

Analyst guidance: if imputation or supplemental data are later authorized, check the
per-source prep datasets (g.prep_mdN) directly.

SECTION 5 -- PCM-D-10 pointer (rt_ANCHOR_to_*_days negatives)
===========================================================================

The following columns legitimately carry negative values and were NOT nulled:

  rt_ANCHOR_to_ADMIT_days
  rt_ANCHOR_to_SURGERY_days
  rt_ANCHOR_to_DISCHG_days

These are offsets from an anchor date, not durations. A negative value means the event
preceded the anchor, which is clinically meaningful. Zero negative values were observed
in the PREP-09 scan (PCM-D-10, resolved 2026-08-27), so no action was taken, but the
design permits negatives by definition.

Do not apply a floor of zero to these variables. See docs/DECISIONS.md PCM-D-10 for the
full triage, including the Bucket-D variables that retain negatives for different reasons.
